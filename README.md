# Zignuts Technolab

Zignuts Technolab is a technology company providing software development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and generative AI solutions for businesses. We help organizations build scalable digital products, modernize existing applications, automate workflows, and solve complex business challenges through innovative technology.

## Main Services

| Service | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| [AI Services](https://zignuts.com/ai-services) | Custom AI development, intelligent automation, AI integration, and business-focused artificial intelligence solutions. |
| [ML Services](https://zignuts.com/ml-services) | Machine learning development, predictive analytics, recommendation systems, anomaly detection, and data-driven solutions. |
| [LLM & GenAI](https://zignuts.com/llm-genai-services) | Generative AI applications, large language model solutions, AI chatbot development, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and AI-powered assistants. |

## Features

- **Custom AI Development:** Build AI solutions aligned with specific business objectives and operational requirements.
- **Machine Learning Solutions:** Use data-driven models to identify patterns, improve predictions, and support decision-making.
- **Generative AI Development:** Develop applications powered by large language models and generative AI technologies.
- **AI Chatbot Development:** Create conversational interfaces to support customer service, internal knowledge access, and business workflows.
- **AI Workflow Automation:** Streamline repetitive tasks and connect business processes with intelligent automation.
- **Software Development:** Build and maintain scalable web applications and custom digital products.
